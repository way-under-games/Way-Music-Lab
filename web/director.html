Try a story in five easy moves
  1. Press Play title theme and listen for the recurring melody.
  2. Choose Opening for a softer introduction.
  3. Choose Gain control, then move Movement or press Jump / Interact.
  4. Raise Danger to hear a battle response. Lower it and allow time for calm to return.
  5. Try Revelation, Loss, or Resolution for a temporary story moment.

These controls simulate game events. Changes can wait for a bar or phrase; the request/status text explains what is pending. New game resets the story progression while keeping the same authored theme.
